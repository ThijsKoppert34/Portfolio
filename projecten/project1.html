PRODUCT DESIGN

Workout Tracker

An intuitive minimalistic application designed to handle complex training routines. Reducing noise during trainings to keep the focus on the workouts. This project was my final thesis project and with this project I developed my passion for UX/UI design. I had to work with multiple stakeholders and did a lot of usability testing. The product will be launched very soon as the developers are now building this design.
